PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION

Your privacy matters to us.

Elle is committed to protecting your personal information. Every order is packed discreetly, and your details are handled with strict confidentiality and security.

DATA & PRIVACY POLICY

How we protect your personal information.

Information we collect

We collect only the details necessary to process your purchase: your name, email address, delivery destination, phone number, and order items. Payment card details and M-Pesa PINs are never handled or stored on our servers.

Discreet packaging

Privacy is our priority. All deliveries arrive in plain, unmarked boxes with no mention of Elle or intimate products on the outer shipping label.

How your data is used

Your details are used strictly to fulfill orders, provide customer support, and communicate tracking updates. If you opt into marketing at checkout, we may send occasional newsletters, which you can opt out of anytime.

Data storage & security

Data is stored securely in encrypted databases. Account passwords are cryptographically hashed using PBKDF2-SHA256 and cannot be viewed by our staff or administrators.

Cookie statement

We use only essential session cookies (such as x_cust_session) to keep you logged in. We do not use third-party tracking, advertising, or cross-site profiling cookies.

Your rights & deletion

You have the right to request a copy of your personal data or request permanent deletion of your account and records. To exercise your rights, contact us at admin@elle.com.
